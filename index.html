Why new homeowners test

Valley water is very hard.

9 to 17grains of hardness per gallon

That's the range in Valley city water quality reports. Anything over about 10.5 grains counts as very hard on the U.S. Geological Survey's scale. Your home's exact number is what the free test tells you.
